About Domesca Homes

Quality over quantity, on every project.

Founded in 2013, Domesca Homes is a Melbourne-based building company specialising in custom homes, renovations, knockdown rebuilds, and multi-unit developments across Melbourne’s north and west.

We were established to meet the growing demand for reliable, high-quality residential construction delivered with clear communication, strong project management, and consistent results.

At Domesca Homes, we focus on quality over quantity. Every project is managed with care from pre-construction through to completion, ensuring attention to detail, transparency, and a smooth building experience for our clients.

We understand the trust involved in building — whether it’s a family home or an investment development. That’s why we take the time to understand your goals, communicate openly, and deliver work that meets both your expectations and industry standards.

Our team works closely with homeowners, investors, and developers to deliver practical building solutions that achieve strong outcomes. From concept through to completion, we are committed to making the process straightforward, professional, and well-managed.

Plans & Design — How We Build

A clear path from first call to handover.

Our design process begins with an initial consultation to understand your vision and requirements. We then collaborate with our partner designers to create a tailored plan, incorporating your preferences and our expertise to ensure functionality and cost-efficiency.

  1. Step 01

    Consultation

    Discuss your project goals, site details, budget and timeline with our team. It’s also your chance to ask questions and understand the next steps.

  2. Step 02

    Design & Planning

    Bring your own plans, or work with our partner designers, architect and interior designer on a concept that balances functionality, style and budget.

  3. Step 03

    Approvals & Permits

    We guide you through the approvals and permit process. Requirements depend on your project, site and local council — we make it clear and manageable.

  4. Step 04

    Construction

    Trades, engineers and designers coordinated under one point of contact, with regular updates on progress and key milestones.

  5. Step 05

    Handover & Warranty

    A thorough final inspection of all aspects of the construction, any adjustments made, then the keys — backed by comprehensive warranty support.

For new homeowners, regular maintenance is key. This includes checking for leaks, cleaning gutters, servicing HVAC systems, and inspecting the roof annually. Domesca Homes also provides guidance on maintaining your home to keep it in top condition.

To maintain energy efficiency, ensure proper insulation, seal windows and doors, and use energy-efficient appliances. Regularly servicing your heating and cooling systems also helps reduce energy consumption.

It is crucial to install paving with a slope away from your house to prevent water accumulation around the foundations. Proper drainage ensures that water flows away from the structure, reducing the risk of damage to the foundation and maintaining the integrity of your home.

It’s recommended to inspect your roof at least twice a year, ideally in the spring and fall. Additionally, check for damage after severe weather events to ensure your roof remains in good condition.

Clean your gutters and downspouts at least twice a year to prevent clogs and water damage. Ensure they are securely attached and direct water away from your home’s foundation.

To prevent mold and mildew, ensure proper ventilation in areas like bathrooms and kitchens. Use dehumidifiers in damp areas and address any water leaks promptly.

Secure outdoor furniture, trim overhanging branches, and ensure windows and doors are properly sealed. For added protection, consider installing storm shutters or impact-resistant windows.

Regularly replace air filters every 1-3 months, schedule annual professional maintenance, and keep the outdoor unit free of debris to ensure your HVAC system operates efficiently.

Regularly clean painted surfaces to remove dirt and grime. Address any peeling or chipping paint promptly and consider applying a fresh coat every 5-10 years, depending on the quality of the paint and exposure to elements.

Clean wooden floors regularly with a soft broom or vacuum designed for hardwood. Avoid excessive water and use a damp mop with a wood-safe cleaner for deeper cleaning.
